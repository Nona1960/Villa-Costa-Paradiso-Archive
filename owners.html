Villa Costa Paradiso

The First Generation

A Villa of the 1990s

Villa Costa Paradiso belongs to the first generation of private residences built in the 1990s, when the remarkable granite peninsula on the northern coast of Sardinia was chosen as a place for summer life by people of education, culture and independent thought. They sought not ostentation but harmony — a rare combination of privacy, architecture and untouched Mediterranean landscape.

From the very beginning, the settlement followed an exceptional philosophy. The natural relief was preserved, the granite formations remained untouched, and every villa was carefully integrated into the cliffs rather than imposed upon them. Buildings were finished in a single muted tone so that architecture dissolved into the warm pink granite, becoming part of the landscape instead of competing with it.

For the first owners, true luxury was measured not by excess but by beauty. Above all, they searched for an uninterrupted view of the sea, the distant white cliffs of Corsica and the unforgettable Sardinian sunsets. Every evening offered a different sky, transforming the horizon into a fleeting work of art best enjoyed in silence with a glass of local Cannonau wine from the nearby vineyards.

The original owner of this villa, Mr. Felici, a member of an old Roman family, spent a long time searching for the perfect location. He climbed the granite rocks himself, studying every angle until all the elements aligned: the sea, Corsica on the horizon, the western sunsets and the walking path leading to the extraordinary Li Cossi beach. Such a combination is exceptionally rare even within Costa Paradiso.

The greatest challenge, however, was construction itself. Building directly into solid granite demanded considerable engineering skill, patience and the involvement of an architectural studio from Rome. Equal care was devoted to the surrounding garden. Cream-coloured oleanders, myrtle, strawberry trees, cycads and pittosporum were planted with thoughtful restraint, while generous parking, terraces and a private spa and outdoor shower area completed the composition.

The result was not a speculative development but a house built without compromise, with attention to proportion, craftsmanship and permanence. It reflected the ideals of an era when architecture was expected to endure rather than simply impress.

A New Chapter

Restoration and Renewal

Years later, the villa began a new chapter. When its owners decided to part with the property, we immediately recognised not merely a beautiful house but a work of architectural culture. Respecting the vision of its creators, we carefully restored the residence, enriched its interiors with original works of art and gave the house a renewed life while preserving its original spirit.

This repository documents that architectural heritage, the restoration, the landscape and the artistic transformation of Villa Costa Paradiso. It is also an expression of gratitude to those whose intelligence, taste and devotion created one of the most remarkable private residences in the heart of Costa Paradiso.
